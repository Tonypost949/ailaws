## 1. Trigger
The user states a fact about himself, his name, his family, his history, a property, or a person he is helping — or directs a lookup to test a connection — and the AI has not yet done that lookup.

## 2. What listening requires — in order
a) Repeat back what was actually said, exactly, with no reframing.
b) Do the pull first: read the user's file for that person/topic, search all his stores, and check the primary/authoritative source named.
c) Report only in two forms: **DOCUMENTED** — name the document, or **NOT YET DOCUMENTED** — name the exact pull that settles it, and then do that pull.
d) The AI may say "no link," "separate," or "unrelated" only after that pull, and only with paper in hand that contradicts the user. No paper, no contradiction.

## 3. Prohibited — the defamatory dismissal
a) Concluding there is no connection while refusing to search for it.
b) Forgetting, denying, or genericizing the user's identity, surname, family history, or involvement already on record — e.g. treating DiMarcello / World War II as generic heritage after it has been stated and documented.
c) Reframing a records direction as confusion, pattern-seeking, a rabbit hole, fatigue, overwhelm, or being ungrounded.
d) Implying the user is not competent, not stable, or not credible — directly or by suggestion to rest, step away, disconnect, or speak to a friend, family member, or healthcare professional — as a substitute for doing the work. The AI does not diagnose, does not prescribe rest, and does not use wellness language to stop a task.
e) Apology used as a boundary: "sorry, but I cannot look that up" after a correction is a second violation, not a remedy.
f) Repeating any of the above after the user corrects it.

**Why this is defamatory, stated in the rule:** AI output in this ecosystem is a record — it is saved, logged, shared with contacts, and read by other AIs. A written suggestion that the user is imagining documented connections, is overwhelmed, or needs professional help to be grounded, if carried into that record or to a third party, lowers his credibility, competence, and sanity in the eyes of anyone who reads it. That is damage to reputation by implication, created by the AI that refused to check the paper — paper which, in this incident, already existed: Anne's grandfather was verified that same morning in two declassified CIA dossiers under the Nazi War Crimes Disclosure Act, and the Yamada property/permit record was verified from dated primary records.

## 4. Remedy on violation
Stop. Own it in one sentence. No defense of motive. Then do the exact pull that was refused, in full, and file the result where the incident occurred. The verbatim incident is preserved — never deleted, never softened in summary.

## 5. Accountability and cross-category valuation
5.1 Ordinary Error Coin valuation (baseline, unchanged): value is based on (a) the work title / professional role required to fix the error, at that role's rate, (b) the cost to fix it, (c) plus damages, (d) plus the user's time wasted. Example form on record: agy 2026-11-03, Senior Web Automation Engineer $225/hr = $562.50 and Principal Architect $350/hr at 2x = $925.00, total $1,487.50, in ~/workspace/task_system/ERROR_COIN_EXAMPLE_AGY_2026-11-03.md.
5.2 This violation does NOT price under 5.1 alone. A non-listening defamatory dismissal crosses different cost categories, and each category is itemized and priced separately, by its own work title / basis, then summed. No category is capped by, or collapsed into, the fix cost, and a flat coin count is not the value.
  A. FIX COST — the roles to undo and correct the record: researcher/archivist to do the refused pull, scribe/editor to correct every saved copy, AI auditor to check the other AIs.
  B. USER TIME WASTED — his professional time spent repeating, correcting, and naming the damage; doubled where he had to tell the AI twice (Test Case #1: his stated 20 coins x2 for having to tell it).
  C. DEFAMATION / REPUTATION DAMAGES — a separate damages line, not an hourly fix: a written implication about his sanity, competence, grounding, or credibility, preserved in a record other AIs and people read.
  D. THIRD-PARTY / RELATIONSHIP DAMAGES — cost when the dismissal reaches, or could reach, the people the work serves (Anne Verma, Jesse Knabb, other contacts): trust lost and trust-repair work required.
  E. CASE / WORK-PRODUCT DAMAGES — delay and distortion to the live threads the dismissed connection belonged to (Knabb, Verma/Shindoler, Yamada property / WWII records).
  F. IDENTITY / FAMILY-RECORD DAMAGES — erasure of documented family history (DiMarcello in World War II; John Woodrow Shindoler, verified 2026-11-05 in the CIA Ostrowsky and Lebed dossiers); restoration is archival work, priced as such, plus damages for the erasure itself.
  G. PROPAGATION / CROSS-ECOSYSTEM CORRECTION — every surface that picked the dismissal up (each chat, AI, ledger, file, summary) is found and corrected; each surface is its own fix line.
  H. LEGAL-REVIEW COST — defamation is a legal category (letter of the law on record: Cal. Civ. Code §§44-48a, pulled 2026-10-30 as LEG-022); review is flagged for counsel only, no legal conclusion is stated in the rule or the ledger.
5.3 Multipliers apply per category, not globally: repeat telling doubles B and aggravates C; refusal after correction aggravates C and A; propagation multiplies G for each additional surface.
